I design small embedded systems and practical printed objects: from an early prototype and enclosure to documented, serviceable hardware.

Typical work includes firmware, controllers, sensor integrations, parametric parts, costume electronics and short-run 3D printing.

Have a strange device or a part that does not exist yet? Send the constraints, rough dimensions and expected use.
